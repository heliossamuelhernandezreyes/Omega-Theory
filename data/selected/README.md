# Selected numerical data

These files are derived from or copied from the frozen Canon Master v1.2 sources.

- The Delivery 19, 20, and 21 CSV files are copied without modification.
- `delivery_18__monotonicity_summary.csv` is an exact aggregate of the original 200-row Delivery 18 table; its source table remains in the frozen archive.

Selection favors compact datasets that support canonical or negative results and are used by the automated validation suite. The complete catalog contains 667 datasets and remains preserved in the master archive.
