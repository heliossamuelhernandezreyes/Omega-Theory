# Omega Research Atlas

Machine- and human-searchable index of research results. This atlas does not promote research-branch claims to canon.

Each phase entry should expose: question; ontology/prior sources; derived equations/structures; assumptions and regime; computational validation status and artifacts; frozen natural result; external evidence/uncertainty used for comparison; epistemic verdict; negative/counterexample results; related phases; commit/path references.

Required validation labels: `FORMAL`, `COMPUTATIONAL`, `FORMAL+COMPUTATIONAL`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE` with reason.

Required epistemic labels where applicable: `DERIVED`, `NUMERICALLY_VERIFIED`, `OBSERVATIONALLY_COMPARED`, `REFUTED`, `UNDERDETERMINED`, `OPEN`.

The atlas is an index, not a substitute for the source reports/results.
