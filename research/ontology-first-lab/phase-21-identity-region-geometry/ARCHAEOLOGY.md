# Archaeology — Phase 21

The canonical and historical corpus contains no closed theory of regions of identity in the Hamming space of compatibility structures.

Phase 19 introduced exact edit distance to a change of Pi_*; Phase 20 suggested interpreting that quantity geometrically. Phase 21 tests that interpretation directly rather than importing a known statistical-mechanics or information-theory construction.

The terms volume, boundary and depth are used combinatorially only. No physical entropy, energy landscape or mass interpretation is assumed.
