Status: experimental, non-canonical.

Question: what is the geometry of regions in graph-space that share the same fixed-point structural partition Pi_*?

Domain: all 4096 directed simple graphs on four labeled nodes, represented as the 12-dimensional Hamming hypercube.

No probabilities, observed constants, masses, energies, or fitted parameters.

Key results:
- 15 labeled Pi_* regions;
- 0/15 regions connected under one-edge edits restricted to the same region;
- depth-to-boundary distribution: 3840 at depth 1, 255 at depth 2, 1 at depth 3;
- whole-partition support of Phase 19 equals depth to region boundary by definition;
- depth, local exposure and regional volume are distinct structural observables.
